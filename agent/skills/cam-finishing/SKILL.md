name: cam-finishing
description: Keep sound, editing and subtitles distinct from visual-film approval.
---
At visual review retain sound, montage and subtitle notes for finishing without blocking unrelated visual work. At finishing inspect current settings and change only supported requested fields. Preserve whole spoken words; use fades at audio boundaries. Unsupported requests remain unresolved, never silently marked applied. A changed final file requires exact-file human approval before download. Technical checks do not prove artistic satisfaction.
