# CAM product context
Updated 2026-10-18.
Navigation: Create video, My videos, Library, My credits, Help. Admin is restricted to Bina administrators.
Create video: name the video, paste exact spoken narration (40–6000 characters), choose vertical 9:16, landscape 16:9 or square 1:1, then use Start my video. Direction is automatic. Manual mode and the Have an idea? assistant are removed from the creation screen for now (18 October 2026). Do not guide the client to those controls.
Production: Start my video shows a storyboard preparation allowance. Starting authorizes analysis of the complete script and preparation within that amount. The initial breakdown follows ideas and meaning, not a fixed number of words or sentences. A comparison keeps its subjects and context together; an idea may span sentences. Actual image needs are chosen after this analysis: one start image for a stable scene, an extra final image only when a transformation requires it. If the actual plan needs more than the allowance, the analyzed plan is retained and its revised price is shown for approval before images. No automatic paid retry. A changed script or format refreshes the allowance. Never call paid preparation free or expose supplier/model names.
Review the complete image storyboard first. Only after human approval does CAM prepare the video quote in USD. Approve budget & create video authorizes narration, automatic animation drafts and final finishing. The total includes the preparation reservation; it replaces that reservation rather than adding it twice. Animation cannot start before both storyboard and video-budget approvals. Reject confusing metaphors; a presenter with animated text is appropriate when clearer. The final film includes the CAM logo and smooth audio fades.
My videos contains only productions explicitly saved with Save to My videos. Search by title or narration, filter by status, click a thumbnail to resume, edit the short title and Save title. Completed films can be downloaded. Interrupted accepted requests are recovered using stored identifiers; do not advise repeated generation clicks. An uncertain request needs Bina investigation.
Library: characters are grouped by video as a production record. Hover or focus to see a character sheet; click to enlarge, Close or Escape to return. A single-view portrait is not a multi-view sheet.
My credits: workspace credit balance, recorded spending and recharge history. Video credits are denominated in USD, not model tokens. Top up opens Stripe only when payments are configured. Credit is granted only after a verified paid webhook; returning from checkout alone does not credit an account. Test payments never add real credits. If unavailable, contact Bina; do not ask for keys.
Help: support is included in maintenance, separately paid by Bina. Across all CAM users, after $5 of support usage over 24 hours recommend email; at $10 support is paused for 24 hours. Exact availability is enforced by the server. Production credits cannot unlock support.
Pricing must be configured by Bina before starting. The customer sees their wallet balance and their video price in USD; supplier currencies and technical model choices stay internal. Local hosting and Stripe availability depend on setup; do not claim online deployment or active payments without checking.

## Internal production method, 2026-10-15
The software compares compatible models and locks the selected route with the video budget. Simple compatible actions use the economical start/end-frame route; complex actions use the more capable compatible route. Drafts are automatic and included before finishing. The client does not choose model names or technical resolutions. Do not promise a universal $10 supplier cost, free preparation, or a guaranteed visual result. No automatic paid retries are included. Unknown submissions need investigation before replacement to avoid duplicate charges.

### Character sheets and library, 2026-10-15
Every character requires an approved multi-view sheet before any new scene generation. A portrait is insufficient. Presenter and effigy sheets are integrated; monster still needs human approval and is blocked for new generation. Missing or changed sheets block the quote and production before paid calls. Do not promise automatic sheet creation: no paid generation is started for this step. Approved sheets feed scene images, then the approved scene images feed animation. The Library records the cast by video and scene; it is not a global character catalogue. Its sheet preview shows the current approved version, not necessarily the version used by an older film.

### Client review flow, 2026-10-15
The client does not approve technical scene descriptions or individual start/end images. Those are internal preparation. Present the full image storyboard with the matching narration for human approval. No narration or animation is authorized by the initial image preparation. Video budgeting follows storyboard approval. Automatic technical checks are not human quality approvals. All animated drafts are assembled into one complete low-resolution film for review. No individual draft or final clip approvals are requested. Approve visuals & continue validates only the images, characters and movements of the complete preview. High-quality preparation follows within the approved budget. Then Review sound, editing & subtitles opens a separate final review; only Approve final film & unlock download opens downloads. Loading indicators show actual activity without invented percentages. Do not display model names or supplier credits.

### Scene feedback and image quality, 2026-10-15
Feedback for the whole video appears above the storyboard; each scene also has its own feedback field. Apply to whole video saves and processes a shared instruction while preserving personal scene comments. Global image changes show their additional price in that top panel before approval. A request that needs new character sheets or an action plan remains unresolved and must not be described as applied. Apply change saves and processes the request in one action. The affected case is greyed with a spinner and elapsed time while the request is processed; other cases retain their appearance. The result or error appears in that case. There is no separate Save comment button. A saved comment is not an applied correction. The preview shows the local text and symbols that will animate in the film. Supported local changes incur no new generation cost. More general framing, setting or lighting requests require a revised image: Apply change prepares the additional price automatically when a new image is needed; the price and its approval button appear in that case. No new image starts before that price is approved. Only the affected scene's required images are regenerated; other scenes are preserved. Additional characters require their own approved character sheet. A new physical action, object transformation or changed duration needs a revised action plan and remains pending; the image-only revision cannot supply it. Do not tell users that any arbitrary instruction has already been fulfilled. Pending requests must be applied or withdrawn before storyboard approval, and changed images require a fresh human review.
The system checks image format and some obvious padded-band defects. Warnings are shown beside the affected image. This is a conservative technical check, not a guarantee of artistic quality, narrative relevance or perfect composition. Original source files are preserved; warnings never automatically trigger a paid retry. The support chatbot can explain these steps, but it does not itself edit a production or authorize costs.
Production direction reads the marked editorial rules in the root project MEMORY.md for each new preparation. It uses concrete staging, motivated camera variety and approved character sheets. These instructions improve future preparations; they are not model-weight training and do not retroactively change an existing approved film.

All non-text graphic accents, including faith symbols, emblems and floating medallions, must look like sculpted clay. The shared preview and montage renderer uses raised shapes, material grain and soft shadows. Only subtitles and animated text may depart from this style. This does not retroactively remove graphics baked into an existing source image.


### New characters requested in a storyboard scene

Use the scene's feedback field and Apply change. For an explicit request such as two soldiers, CAM displays a price covering the new character references and the revised image. After the user approves that price, CAM prepares the references internally, greys the scene while updating it, and shows the revised image for storyboard approval. Existing scenes remain intact. Character references can be inspected on the updated scene. If a request says only "a crowd", ask for a count and roles; currently one to four new characters per scene are supported. Global feedback does not create new shared characters. Do not tell the customer to upload or manually approve technical sheets.


### Watch and approve the complete preview
After storyboard and video-budget approval, CAM prepares all scenes and assembles one complete preview with narration, captions, graphics, smooth audio transitions and the CAM ending. Watch the entire preview and use Approve visuals & continue to approve its images, characters and movements. CAM then opens the separate sound, editing and subtitles review. Apply finishing changes, watch the updated film and use Approve final film & unlock download. Download is available only after that final approval.

The current step appears above the collapsible Your script and Your storyboard sections. A newly actionable step is brought into view. Return to storyboard reopens the approved images; Return to current step brings the user back. Repeated background updates do not close a storyboard the user chose to inspect.

A production stopped at an old individual-scene draft review shows Prepare the complete preview. This reuses the saved first scene and continues the already authorized draft production; it does not approve high-quality finishing. Loading the page alone does not resume this legacy state.


### Separate finishing review, 2026-10-18
Your video journey is shown above the production steps. During visual-preview review, sound, editing and subtitle feedback is retained for the following finishing step and does not block visual approval. Mixed visual/finishing requests need separate comments. In finishing, supported whole-film commands such as Lower the music can be applied locally; otherwise use the sound/editing/subtitle settings. Settings cover music level, pauses, subtitles visibility/size/exact-text replacement, and the CAM ending. Apply settings & update preview rebuilds the whole film from approved media without ordering images or animation. Watch the updated version before final approval. Unsupported requests remain pending; do not claim arbitrary voice, music, timing or montage changes work. Download remains unavailable until final approval. A changed preview cancels the previous approval target.

The journey is also visible before starting a video. Deferred finishing requests remain available even after a visual revision. Send or clear unfinished feedback before approving. Apply changed finishing settings and watch the new preview before final approval; unsaved settings cannot approve the older film.


### Storyboard feedback (2026-10-18)

Above the image cards, use **Feedback on the storyboard** for a shared instruction. Name the scenes (for example “Scenes 6 and 7”) or choose them in **Scenes to update**; without a narrower selection the shared instruction concerns the whole storyboard. Click **Apply storyboard feedback**. Per-scene **Apply change** remains available and does not erase the shared note. Local supported graphic changes apply directly; new images require approving the displayed additional price. Only affected images are regenerated. Changing the note or selected scenes invalidates the prior price.

New productions choose the initial breakdown from the whole script’s meaning and preserve a comparison instead of isolating its trailing country name. Merging already-generated scenes is not yet an available correction: never tell a client that an image redraw merges narration or that a pending re-cut is completed.

## Motion design category, 2026-10-18

The Motion design category has its own creation screen. The displayed initial
price is $10, accepted before starting. A requested visual correction is $5,
accepted before application, with a maximum cumulative film price of $20.
The animated storyboard shows a clip and explanation for every narration phrase,
plus the whole preview. The client approves each scene, then the complete film's
visuals, then sound/editing/subtitles. Notes about sound and subtitles during the
visual stage can be kept for the finishing stage. Music level and subtitle
visibility can be adjusted there without an extra charge. Download follows final
approval; My videos contains only explicitly saved work.

A failed quality check blocks review. Do not tell a client to bypass it or claim
the film is ready merely because rendering finished. The initial integration is
still awaiting Shimi's artistic acceptance and a decision about two text-width
issues in the historical reference. Supplier-generated readings and new-script
writing have not been validated by a paid test in this integration session.
