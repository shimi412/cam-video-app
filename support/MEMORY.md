# CAM product context
Updated 2026-10-14.
Navigation: Create video, My videos, Library, My credits, Help. Admin is restricted to Bina administrators.
Create video: name the video, paste exact spoken narration (40–6000 characters), choose vertical 9:16, landscape 16:9 or square 1:1. The expandable assistant can develop an idea; Use this narration copies its proposal into the script. The discussion stays linked to the video. Add references or explore versions opens its project workspace for image/PDF references and versions. Links are saved, not automatically browsed.
Prepare scenes with Claude prepares the scene plan, currently up to $0.25 per analysis from the assistant budget. Media generation requires a separate quote and explicit approval. Never describe paid preparation as free.
Production: Get current quote reads prices without generating media. Review the displayed budget before approving. Review the complete narration, then scene images. A human must validate the storyboard before animation. Reject confusing visual metaphors; a presenter with animated text is appropriate when clearer. The final film includes a CAM logo ending and smooth audio fades. A quote is an estimate, not a supplier invoice.
My videos: search by title or narration, filter by status, click a thumbnail to resume, edit the short title and Save title. Completed films can be downloaded. Interrupted accepted requests are recovered using stored identifiers; do not advise repeated generation clicks. An uncertain request needs Bina investigation.
Library: hover or focus a character to see its full reference; click to enlarge, Close or Escape to return. References are original assets, not generated multi-angle sheets.
My credits: workspace credit balance, recorded spending and recharge history. Video credits are denominated in USD, not model tokens. Top up opens Stripe only when payments are configured. Credit is granted only after a verified paid webhook; returning from checkout alone does not credit an account. Test payments never add real credits. If unavailable, contact Bina; do not ask for keys.
Help: support is included in maintenance, separately paid by Bina. Across all CAM users, after $5 of support usage over 24 hours recommend email; at $10 support is paused for 24 hours. Exact availability is enforced by the server. Production credits cannot unlock support.
Customer production approval remains unavailable until Bina activates pricing. Administrator operator screens can show supplier quotes in dollars and separate supplier credits. Do not convert them or promise a fixed exchange rate. Local hosting and Stripe availability depend on administrator setup; do not claim the service is deployed online.
